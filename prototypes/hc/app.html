Holdings Collection redesign

A clickable prototype: the client connects their banks with BankID and shares holdings with their advisor, and the advisor gets a report.

How to use it

  • The Preview panel on the left is not part of the product. Pick a flow, its version and any screen under State. Collapse it with the icon in its corner.
  • Everything is clickable. Tap a bank to open BankID, then Same Device simulates a successful sign-in. Closing the BankID window cancels that bank.
  • Standalone is the link the advisor sends to the client. PTN is the same flow inside the PTN iframe: Welcome instead of the start screen, Next instead of sharing, no report.
  • Display at the bottom of the panel switches Web and Mobile, and the dark theme.

What to look at

  1. 1Steps: pick banksThe main proposal. Tap a bank card, sign in, then Review connections.
  2. 2Steps: several banks at onceBank selection, Several, then Review. Tick banks and Connect: BankID opens for each in turn. Compare Queue display: Top pill and Side panel.
  3. 3Steps: connection statusesHover the bank logos in the bottom bar to see the status of each bank.
  4. 4Steps: reviewThe last check: remove a bank, + Add bank, add manual accounts, then Share with advisor.
  5. 5One screenThe alternative: search, favourites and connected banks on one screen.
  6. 6ReportWhat the advisor gets. Compare Charts: Bars and Pies + map, hover the charts, see the new Fees section.

What changed after the feedback

  • Facelift: bank cards with logos and account types, one clear main action per screen, client details only on the start screen.
  • Fewer clicks: tapping a bank opens BankID right away, there is no separate add step before signing in.
  • Fewer choices at once: the Other options modal is gone, one feedback link sits under the main button, in Steps manual accounts move to Review.
  • One bank or several: both work in Steps; several banks connect in a queue, and its progress is shown next to the provider's BankID window without changing it.
  • Clear statuses: each bank's status in the bar hint, in the queue and on Review; problems come with Try again.
  • Manual accounts: creating an account and adding holdings are separate steps, both editable in place.
  • Report: the prod content in the Portfolio Builder style, pie charts and a region map with hover, a new Fees section (fund fees, funds above 1%, cost over 10 years), missing data shown as a warning in each section, Download PDF always at hand.

Decisions we need
